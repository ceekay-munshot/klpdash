> Every price below is a **real seller ASK/listing price** we pulled from the web — MOQ-dependent, usually ex-GST & ex-freight, **not** a negotiated bulk quote. Nothing is estimated or made up; where a seller hides the price we say "ask." Real bulk quotes usually land a bit below these asks.

## 1) DEXTROSE (Anhydrous IP)
- **Market ask: ₹60–81/kg** (typical ~₹66–73). Export/wholesale floor ~₹43–52/kg.
- **You pay: ₹66–75/kg → FAIR, even slightly favourable** for true IP grade.
- Cheap ₹40–42 quotes = no grade stated (industrial/food, not IP) — ignore as comparables.
- **Real sellers:** Hatchx (IP, ₹66), Mahalaxmi Starch *maker* (₹65), Shree Radhey Krishna *maker* (₹60), Jinkrupa (₹68), Neha/"Universal" (IP, ₹81).
- **Where your saving is:** internal — Sayaji ₹66.6 vs Gujarat ₹68.5 vs Universal ₹75.5, + plant leveling (₹23 L).

## 2) LAMINATES (pharma/ORS sachet film)
- **Market ask: ₹205–305/kg (avg ~₹250).** Cheaper commodity metallised-PET film ₹120–220 (lighter, lower barrier — different grade). Heavy Alu-foil ₹375–490.
- **You pay: ₹250–302/kg → FAIR** (top of the fair band for a true multi-ply PET/Alu/PE sachet laminate).
- **Real sellers:** N Flex Systems (₹260–305, best match), Sampurna (₹250), AV Super (₹205), Eternal (₹200–230), Patidar metallised-PET (₹145).
- **Where your saving is:** ONLY if your product is actually a lighter metallised-PET grade → then push toward ₹200–230. **Must confirm your exact structure (gauge spec).** Otherwise you're priced right.

## 3) PACKAGING (28mm induction-wad PP cap + bottles)
- **28mm pharma seal cap market ask: ₹0.55–1.20/pc (typical ~₹0.85–0.95).**
- **You pay: ₹0.8–1.1/pc → FAIR, not overpaying.**
- **Modest headroom:** pharma seal caps list as low as ₹0.54–0.75 (Hads Pack ₹0.54, Indore Pack EPE-liner ₹0.75); at higher volume / looser spec **₹0.65–0.80 looks achievable.**
- ⚠️ **Key check:** confirm your ₹0.8–1.1 is for the **complete capped + wadded closure** (fair) — NOT just the wad insert (which alone is only ₹0.17–0.80/pc). CRC child-resistant caps (₹3.25) are a different product — don't compare.
- **Other items (market ask):** HDPE bottles ₹4–5/pc · PET bottles ₹2.5–5 · PET preforms ₹125–130/kg · measuring cups ₹0.25–0.50 · LDPE droppers ₹2–2.5.
- **Real sellers:** Hads Pack (₹0.54), Indore Pack (₹0.75), Dewan Polycaps (₹0.85), Kevalin PET bottles (₹1.60/2.45), Ujval cups (₹0.18–0.50).

## HONESTY / CAVEATS
- IndiaMART blocked direct fetch (429) → its numbers are search snapshots (confirm by phone); TradeIndia/ExportersIndia/GlobalLinker read live.
- Asks are ex-GST (~18%), ex-freight, MOQ-dependent. "Pharma" on a listing is a claim — demand a compliance certificate.
